Privacy Policy

Quick Hire Recruitment Solutions

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Quick Hire Recruitment Solutions ("Quick Hire", "we", "us", or "our") handles personal information submitted by people applying for or asking about job opportunities through our recruitment services, social media forms, messaging channels, or other communications.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information you choose to provide, including your name, phone number, email address, CV or résumé, education, work history, language proficiency, availability, screening answers, and messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use applicant information to review applications, assess qualifications for advertised roles, contact applicants, arrange interviews, share relevant opportunities, coordinate recruitment, maintain recruitment records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

3. Sharing with Hiring Companies

When relevant to a vacancy, we may share the information you submit with the company recruiting for that role so it can evaluate your application and contact you. Applications for vacancies advertised for Telephonic may be shared with its recruitment or hiring team. We do not sell applicant personal information.

4. Service Providers

We may use third-party services to manage recruitment and communications, such as social media lead forms, email, spreadsheets, and messaging platforms. These providers may process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain applicant information for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, recordkeeping, and legal purposes. We use reasonable measures designed to protect personal information, although no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your Choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, or ask us to stop contacting you about opportunities. Requests are subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate recordkeeping needs. Contact us through the official channel listed on the recruitment advertisement or the channel you used to apply.

7. Children

Our recruitment services are intended for people legally eligible to apply for the relevant employment opportunities.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. Updates will be reflected on this page.

9. Contact Us

For questions or requests regarding applicant information, contact Quick Hire Recruitment Solutions through the official contact channel listed on the relevant recruitment advertisement or the channel through which you applied.
